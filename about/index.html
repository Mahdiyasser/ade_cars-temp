Our Journey

Company Milestones

Fifteen years of passion, growth, and dedication to making luxury accessible.

2009
Founded

The Beginning

Adeoti Motors opens its first showroom in Lagos with a small but handpicked selection of premium vehicles, built on a vision of honest, transparent automotive retail.

First 100 Sales

Within two years, Adeoti Motors celebrates its 100th vehicle sold — a milestone driven by word-of-mouth referrals and a reputation for integrity.

2011
Milestone
2014
Expansion

Service Centre Launch

We expanded beyond sales with a fully equipped service and maintenance centre, offering repairs, inspections and routine upkeep under one roof.

Luxury Fleet Upgrade

Adeoti Luxury Motors officially launches its premium tier, onboarding hypercars and exotic vehicles to cater to high-net-worth clientele across Nigeria.

2018
Premium
2021

Launched our digital showroom, enabling customers across Africa to browse, enquire and reserve vehicles — bringing luxury car buying to their fingertips.
